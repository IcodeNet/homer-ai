# Licences of the Homer texts

The **MIT licence in the project's [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) does not apply to this folder.**
It covers the code and the course text only. The texts in `data/homer/` keep their own licences:

| Files | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `odyssey/greek/`, `iliad/greek/` | Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University | **CC BY-SA 4.0** (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ |
| `odyssey/english/`, `iliad/english/` | Samuel Butler's prose translations (1900 and 1898) | **Public domain** |

- **Greek (CC BY-SA 4.0).** You must credit the Perseus Digital Library and link to it, and if you share changed versions of the Greek text you must share them under the same licence.
  The exact credit, the editions and the download details are in [README.md](README.md#sources-and-licences).
- **English (public domain).** Butler died in 1902 and both books were published before 1930. The Project Gutenberg header, footer and licence text were removed from our copy.
