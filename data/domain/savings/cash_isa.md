# Cash ISA Saver

Product: cash-isa
Version: 2
In force from: 2026-04-06
Note: invented sample data for a course. Example Bank does not exist.

## 1. What this account is

An ISA (Individual Savings Account) for cash. Interest on money held in an ISA is paid free of UK income tax. The rate is variable.

## 2. Who can open it

UK residents aged 18 or over. An ISA is always in one name; there are no joint ISAs.

## 3. Paying in

You can open the account with £1. Each tax year you can pay in up to the yearly ISA allowance, which is set by the government and can change. This sheet does not quote the figure: check the government's own website.

## 4. Withdrawals

You can take money out at any time with no notice. This is not a flexible ISA. If you take money out and later pay it back in, the money you pay back counts towards your yearly allowance again.

## 5. Transfers in

To move an ISA from another provider to us, fill in our ISA transfer form and we arrange the rest. Do not withdraw the money yourself: money that leaves an ISA any other way loses its tax-free status. We aim to finish a transfer within 15 working days.

## 6. Transfers out

To move this ISA to another provider, ask the new provider to arrange it. We make no charge.

## 7. Interest

Interest is worked out every day and paid once a year, on 5 April. Current and past rates are in the rates table, not in this sheet. We give you at least 14 days' notice before we lower the rate.
