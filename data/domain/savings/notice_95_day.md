# 95-Day Notice Saver

Product: notice-95
Version: 4
In force from: 2026-09-01
Note: invented sample data for a course. Example Bank does not exist.

## 1. What this account is

A savings account where you tell us 95 days ahead before you take money out. In return the rate is usually higher than on an account with instant access. The rate is variable.

## 2. Who can open it

UK residents aged 18 or over. The account is in one name only.

## 3. Paying in

You need £500 to open the account. The most you can hold is £250,000. You can add money at any time.

## 4. Withdrawals

To take money out you give us 95 days' notice, online or by secure message. We send the money to your nominated account on the day the notice ends. You can cancel a notice at any time before it ends.

## 5. If you need your money sooner

There is no access without notice. If you need money sooner because of serious illness or money trouble, contact us. A member of staff looks at each case; this assistant cannot decide it.

## 6. Interest

Interest is worked out every day and paid once a year, on the anniversary of the day the account opened. Money under notice keeps earning interest until it leaves. Before we lower the rate we give you at least 109 days' notice: 14 days plus the 95-day notice period.

## 7. Closing the account

Closing the account is a withdrawal of everything, so it needs 95 days' notice too.
