# 1-Year Fixed Saver

Product: fixed-1-year
Version: 2
In force from: 2026-08-15
Note: invented sample data for a course. Example Bank does not exist.

## 1. What this account is

A savings account for a lump sum that you leave with us for a term of 12 months. The interest rate is fixed for the whole term.

## 2. Who can open it

UK residents aged 18 or over. The account is in one name only.

## 3. Paying in

The smallest deposit is £1,000 and the largest is £250,000. You have 14 days after the account opens to pay your money in. After those 14 days nothing more can be added.

## 4. Withdrawals

There are no withdrawals during the 12-month term, and the account cannot be closed early. The one exception is the death of the account holder (section 7).

## 5. Interest

The rate is fixed. You get the rate that was in force on the day your account opened, and it stays the same until the term ends. Rates by date are in the rates table, not in this sheet. Interest is paid once, at the end of the term.

## 6. When the term ends

We write to you 21 days before the term ends to ask what you want to do. If we hear nothing from you, your money and its interest move to an Easy Access Saver in your name.

## 7. If the account holder dies

The person dealing with the estate can close the account before the term ends, free of charge. Our bereavement team handles this in person, not through this assistant.

## 8. Changing your mind

You can cancel within 14 days of opening, as long as you have not paid any money in yet.
