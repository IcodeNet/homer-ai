# Easy Access Saver

Product: easy-access
Version: 3
In force from: 2026-09-01
Note: invented sample data for a course. Example Bank does not exist.

## 1. What this account is

A savings account you can pay into and take money out of whenever you like. The interest rate is variable, which means it can go up or down.

## 2. Who can open it

UK residents aged 18 or over. You can hold one Easy Access Saver in your own name. Two people can also open one together as a joint account.

## 3. Paying in

You can open the account with £1. The most you can hold is £250,000. Pay in by bank transfer from your nominated account, as often as you like.

## 4. Withdrawals

You can take money out at any time, with no notice and no charge. We send it to your nominated account. Ask before 3pm on a working day and it arrives the same day. Ask after 3pm and it arrives the next working day.

## 5. Interest

Interest is worked out every day and paid monthly, on the first working day of each month. The rate is variable. Current and past rates are in the rates table, not in this sheet. We give you at least 14 days' notice before we lower the rate.

## 6. Closing the account

You can close the account at any time, free of charge. We pay the interest earned up to the day before it closes.

## 7. Changing your mind

You have 14 days from the day the account opens to cancel it. We return your money with the interest it earned.
