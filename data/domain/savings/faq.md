## 1. What is a nominated account, and how do I change it?

Your nominated account is the one bank account in your own name that all your withdrawals go to. To change it, send us a secure message or the change form. We write to your home address to confirm, and the change takes 3 working days.

## 2. How do I change my address?

Sign in and edit your profile, or send us a secure message. We send a letter to both the old and the new address.

## 3. I have forgotten my password

Use the reset link on the sign-in page. After 5 wrong attempts your online access is locked for your safety, and you need to call us to unlock it.

## 4. When do I get my yearly statement?

We send a statement of the interest you earned each April, by secure message. You can ask for a paper copy.

## 5. How do I make a complaint?

Tell us by phone, by secure message or by letter. A person in our complaints team deals with every complaint; this assistant does not. We acknowledge a complaint within 3 working days.

## 6. Someone has died. What should I do?

We are sorry for your loss. Please contact our bereavement team by phone or letter. They tell you which documents are needed and deal with the accounts in person. There is no charge.

## 7. Can you tell me which account is best for me?

No. We do not give financial advice. We can explain how each account works, and the choice is yours. For advice, speak to a financial adviser.

## 8. Can someone else manage my account for me?

Yes, with a registered power of attorney. Send us the document and proof of identity for the attorney. A member of staff checks it and sets up their access.

## 9. Can I get letters in large print, or other support?

Yes. Tell us what you need: large print, more time, or contact by phone only. We keep a note of it so that you do not have to ask twice.

## 10. When are your phone lines open?

Monday to Friday from 8am to 6pm, and Saturday from 9am to 1pm. They are closed on Sundays and bank holidays.
