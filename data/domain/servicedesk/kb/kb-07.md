# A printer shows as offline

ID: kb-07
Category: hardware
Last reviewed: 2026-07-15
Note: invented sample data for a course.

Check that the printer is switched on and shows no paper jam. On your laptop, remove the printer and add it again from the list of office printers; you must be on the Staff network or the VPN. If everyone on the floor sees it offline, report the printer's name from its label to the service desk.
