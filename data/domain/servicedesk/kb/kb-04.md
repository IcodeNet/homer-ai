# Office Wi-Fi drops or will not connect

ID: kb-04
Category: network
Last reviewed: 2026-08-30
Note: invented sample data for a course.

Use the Staff network, not Guest. Forget the network on your laptop, then join again with your work account. If the signal drops in one meeting room only, tell the service desk which room: an access point may be faulty. Wired desks can use the network cable in the docking station instead.
