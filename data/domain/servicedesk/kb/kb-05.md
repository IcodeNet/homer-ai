# A shared mailbox does not show in your mail app

ID: kb-05
Category: email
Last reviewed: 2026-09-02
Note: invented sample data for a course.

A shared mailbox appears by itself within an hour of access being granted. If it does not, close and reopen the mail app. Still missing? Add it by hand: Account settings, Add shared mailbox, and type its address. If you get a permission error, the owner of the mailbox has to approve your access first.
