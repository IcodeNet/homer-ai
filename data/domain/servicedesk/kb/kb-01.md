# Reset your password

ID: kb-01
Category: access
Last reviewed: 2026-10-05
Note: invented sample data for a course.

Go to the sign-in page and choose Forgot password. Enter your work email address. A reset link arrives within a few minutes; it works for one hour. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters that you have not used before. If no link arrives, check your junk folder, then ask the service desk to unlock your account.
