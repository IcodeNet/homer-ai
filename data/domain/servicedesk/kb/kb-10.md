# Report a suspicious email

ID: kb-10
Category: security
Last reviewed: 2026-10-28
Note: invented sample data for a course.

If an email asks for your password, pushes you to act fast, or comes from an address that is almost right, do not click its links or open its attachments. Use the Report phishing button in the mail app, which sends it to the security team and removes it. If you already clicked or typed your password, change your password now and call the service desk.
