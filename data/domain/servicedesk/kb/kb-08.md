# Your laptop is slow or the fan never stops

ID: kb-08
Category: hardware
Last reviewed: 2026-09-25
Note: invented sample data for a course.

Restart the laptop: many are only ever put to sleep, and updates wait for a restart. Close browser tabs you do not need. Open the task manager and look for one program using most of the processor or memory. If the laptop is more than four years old, ask the service desk to check whether it is due for replacement.
