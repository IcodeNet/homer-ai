# Your mailbox is full

ID: kb-06
Category: email
Last reviewed: 2026-10-20
Note: invented sample data for a course.

A mailbox holds 50 GB. When it is nearly full you get a warning, and at the limit you cannot send. Empty Deleted Items and Junk first. Then turn on the online archive: mail older than two years moves there by itself and stays searchable. Large attachments belong on the shared drive, not in mail.
