# Connect to the VPN from home

ID: kb-03
Category: network
Last reviewed: 2026-10-12
Note: invented sample data for a course.

The VPN (virtual private network) is needed for the finance system and the shared drives when you are outside the office. Open the VPN client, choose the Office profile and sign in with your work account and an MFA code. If it stops at Connecting, restart the client and check that your home internet works. The VPN does not work from guest networks that block it, such as some hotels.
